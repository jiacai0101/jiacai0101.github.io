An integrated platform for screening frequent false positives in chemical biology and drug discovery.

## Highlights

- Built with multitask DMPNN models and more than 820,000 compounds.
- Integrates seven categories of false-positive mechanisms, 1,441 alert substructures, and ten filtering rules.
- Supports batch evaluation and API access for scalable screening.
