A web-based tool for substructure modification to improve ADMET properties of lead compounds.

## Highlights

- Integrates 41,779 experimentally verified and 146,450 predicted chemical transformation rules.
- Covers 32 ADMET properties for optimization analysis.
- Provides ADMET profiling and synthetic accessibility estimates for optimized molecules.
