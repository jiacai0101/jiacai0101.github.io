A comprehensive online ADMET prediction platform with API access and decision support for drug discovery.

## Highlights

- Covers 119 endpoints and more than 400,000 entries according to the CV.
- Built on a multitask DMPNN framework with molecular descriptors.
- Supports uncertainty estimation and API-based workflow integration.
