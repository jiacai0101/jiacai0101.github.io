A free drug interaction platform covering drug-drug, drug-food, and drug-disease interactions.

## Highlights

- Covers 2,310 drugs and 302,516 interaction records.
- Adds mechanism descriptions, management advice, and visualization tools.
- Designed to support clinical decision making and patient safety.
